#1
Healthy break box
Adeaze O.
My idea is called... Healthy break box
The problem it solves is... Some students at my school get hungry during the day, but the easiest snacks to buy are unhealthy sweets and sugary drinks.
The people it helps are... It helps students who need a healthy snack during break, especially students who stay at school for a long day.
The solution is... I will make snack boxes with cheap healthy foods like fruit slices, roasted peanuts, boiled eggs, or yoghurt. Students can order a box before school and collect it at break.
The way it could earn or save money to support itself is... Students can pay a small amount for each snack box. I can use the money to buy more ingredients from local fruit sellers and offer a discount for students who order for the full week.
The resources I can start with are... I can start by asking the local fruit sellers which fruits are the cheapest and healthiest. I can clean and reuse plastic containers. I can ask a few classmates to help pack the snack boxes.
The first small step is... I will ask 10 learners what healthy snacks they would actually buy and find out the prices from two local sellers.
#2
Taka-Safi Eco-Blocks
Akira N.
My idea is called... Taka-Safi Eco-Blocks
The problem it solves is... It solves the problem of plastic pollution in our environment and reduces the spread of malaria by keeping our water trenches clear so water can flow.
The people it helps are... It helps our neighbors, especially young children and pregnant mothers who are most affected by malaria, and the local clinic which is always overcrowded.
The solution is... My friends and I will collect the discarded plastic bottles and soft polythene bags from the trenches and roadsides. We will wash them and pack the soft plastics very tightly into the bottles using wooden sticks to create strong "eco-bricks." We will use mud or cement to join them together to build things like school dustbins, benches, and small walls for chicken houses.
The way it could earn or save money to support itself is... We can sell the finished eco-bricks to local builders, farmers, or NGOs who want cheap and strong building materials. It will also save money for our community because families will not have to spend all their savings buying malaria drugs at the pharmacy.
The resources I can start with are... I can start with things that are free around me: the scattered plastic bottles, the polythene bags, long sticks from the trees in our school compound for packing, and the hands of my fellow students.
The first small step is... To go to the staff room tomorrow during break time and present my idea to our Agriculture teacher, asking for permission to set up a small collection point for plastic bottles behind our classrooms.
#3
Stress free tests
Ngozi Okonko
My idea is called... Stress free tests
The problem it solves is... Some students feel stressed before tests and exams. They worry about forgetting what they studied or not doing well.
The people it helps are... It helps students who feel nervous before tests, especially students who do not always have someone to ask for help and struggle with their mental health.
The solution is... I will print out notebooks that have some encouraging quotes and study tips on each page. The notebook will also include a place to make a study checklist and blank pages to make revision notes.
The way it could earn or save money to support itself is... I can make one sample notebook and show it to students before printing many copies. If students like it, they can order one and pay a small deposit to help cover the printing cost. Once I have enough orders, I can print the notebooks in bulk for a cheaper price. Students can pay the rest when they get their notebook.
The resources I can start with are... I can use the printer in the school library to print out one sample notebook to show students. My cousin has a laptop and can help me design the notebook and find quotes to put on the pages.
The first small step is... I will find quotes and tips that can help people feel more calm when they are stressed on the internet using my cousin's laptop.
