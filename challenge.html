What is a Quest?

Each Quest has three Activities: Discover, Explore and Reflect. You must finish them in that order. You will earn points for each Activity. If you earn enough points, you will unlock the next Activity. Once you have finished all three Activities, you have completed an entire Quest.

Do you have more questions? We have answers!

How do I earn points?

You earn points by taking part in the Wavumbuzi Challenge.

You earn points when you:

  • answer questions
  • finish Activities
  • complete Quests

Your points help you move up the leaderboard and unlock new parts of the Challenge.

How can I get as many points as possible?

To get more points, keep taking part in the Challenge and try your best.

You can:

  • complete more Quests
  • answer questions carefully in Discover activities
  • play and replay Explore activities to improve your score
  • complete Reflect activities when they unlock

The more you take part, the more chances you have to learn, improve, and earn points.

Where can I see how many points I have?

You can see your points on the Challenge leaderboard.

During the pre-challenge warm-up period, From February to April, the leaderboard shows the points learners are already earning by exploring Discover activities.

During the main Challenge, from May to June, your points can help you move into the bronze, silver, or gold league of the leaderboard. Aim for the gold league to earn your 2028 Challenge gold league badge!

Your points may take a little time to update, so check again later if you do not see them right away.

What can I win in the Challenge?

You can earn points, badges, profile rewards, and a bronze, silver, or gold league place on the leaderboard.

Some learners, clubs, schools, or Labs teams may also win awards or prizes.

The prizes may be different each year, so ask your teacher or Wavumbuzi contact what prizes are available in this Challenge.

Will I get a badge and a place on the leaderboard if I only finish Discover?

Yes, Discover can help you earn points and appear on the leaderboard during the pre-challenge and main Challenge periods.

You can also earn a theme badge if you reach the points target for that theme. Your badge can grow from bronze to silver to gold as you earn more points on the leaderboard.

To unlock Explore, Reflect, and Labs, you will need to reach the next points target and keep going.

How can I start a Wavumbuzi Club at my school?

A Wavumbuzi Club is a wonderful way for classmates to work together to learn skills and earn points. Learn how to set goals and achieve them as a team. If you want to start your own Wavumbuzi Club, read this guide on Starting a club to learn how.
